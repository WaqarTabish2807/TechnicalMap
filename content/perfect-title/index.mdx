## The Anatomy of a Winning Title

### 1. The "Power Words"
Notice how the score jumps when you add words like **"Guide"**, **"Tutorial"**, or **"vs"**. Technical readers are looking for specific formats. They don't want "Musings on React"; they want "The Ultimate Guide to React Performance".

### 2. The SEO Sweet Spot
Google Search results typically truncate titles after **60 characters**.
*   **Too Short**: You aren't giving enough context.
*   **Too Long**: Your key value proposition might get cut off in the SERP (Search Engine Results Page).

Aim for that 40-60 character sweet spot where you maximize keywords without getting truncated.

### 3. Be Specific
"JavaScript Tips" is a bad title. "10 Advanced JavaScript Tips for Senior Engineers" is a winning title. Specificity breeds confidence.
